# Product language and climate-claim rules

- Call calculated results **estimated avoided emissions**, never “carbon credits,” “Scope 3 deductions,” “carbon offsets,” “tax write-offs,” “legally binding,” “pre-audited,” or “third-party verified.”
- Describe uploaded documents as **consistency-checked and physically reconciled**, never authenticated.
- Show low/base/high values, factor source and version, displacement, retention, handling, eligible units, and sample sizes wherever an estimate is explained.
- Treat `NO_ANSWER` as not retained in the base estimate. Do not imply that every distributed item creates a climate benefit.
- State what is simulated (carrier, scale, payment, time) and what is sourced (methodology references) in the UI and docs.
- Recycling is the recommended route for broken, unsafe, recalled, or low-demand goods. EcoDrops is for safe, functional surplus.
